退款政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日

购买后 7 天内提出申请，且未使用该订单的付费权益，可申请全额退款。重复扣款、付款后未开通或服务故障会单独核查处理，不受这项普通退款条件简单限制。

1. 7 天退款规则

本政策适用于通过 Texta 正式购买的按月 Plus、按月 Pro、永久 Plus 和永久 Pro。自订单支付成功起 7 × 24 小时内，向客服提出申请，且尚未使用该订单对应的付费生成权益，可申请全额退款。仅登录、查看套餐或浏览已有资料，不视为使用付费生成权益。

退款资格会结合订单时间、开通情况和相关生成记录核查。请在发现购买错误时停止继续使用付费权益并及时联系。已使用付费权益或超过 7 天的普通“改变主意”申请，将结合具体情况评估，不承诺自动退款；适用法律赋予的退货、撤回或其他退款权利不受影响。

2. 扣款或服务异常

  • 重复扣款或重复购买：提供相关订单，我们会核查并协调退回重复收取的金额。
  • 付款后未开通：先检查发起购买的 Texta 账户及支付确认状态；若订单已付款而权益未交付，我们会补开通或协调退款。
  • 服务故障导致购买权益无法正常使用：提供发生时间和问题描述，我们会调查并按影响程度协商修复、补偿或相应退款。
  • 疑似盗刷或未经授权交易：尽快联系 FastSpring 买家支持和你的支付机构，并同时告知 Texta。

3. 如何申请

请发送邮件至 1963372275@qq.com，主题建议为“Texta 退款申请”，并提供：

  • Texta 注册邮箱；
  • FastSpring 订单编号或订单确认邮件中的参考号；
  • 购买的套餐、付款时间及金额；
  • 退款原因，以及是否已使用该订单的付费权益。

也可通过 FastSpring Buyer Support 提交付款或退款问题。请勿发送完整银行卡号、安全码、支付宝密码或身份证照片；通常订单编号与账户邮箱足以开始核查。

4. 退款方式与权益调整

Texta 核查权益和申请条件，并与 FastSpring 协调退款。FastSpring 作为交易销售方处理实际退款，原则上通过原支付渠道退回；到账时间取决于 FastSpring、支付方式及银行处理，不能保证即时到账。支付宝等实时支付方式的退款需要 FastSpring 支持团队处理。

核查结果或退款进度会通过邮件告知。如需要更多订单信息，我们会说明原因。退款获确认后，该笔购买所授予的权益将被撤销或调整；此前独立购买且未退款的有效权益不因此失效。部分退款如适用，将先说明金额与对应权益处理方式。

5. 付款条款与消费者权利

FastSpring 的 FastSpring Terms of Sale 及适用的地区补充条款管理付款交易。本政策是 Texta 对服务问题及自愿退款的说明，不能限制强制性消费者权利，也不能阻止 FastSpring 根据其规则处理欺诈、错误扣款或争议退款。

按月套餐均为一次性支付，不自动续费，所以无需取消自动扣款。永久套餐一次性支付、无预设到期日，不等于任何情况下均不可退款。当前网站购买尚未开放；Test 测试订单不发生真实扣款，不适用真实资金退款。

Refund Policy

Effective and updated: October 5, 2026

Eligibility, how to contact us, and how refunds are processed.

On this page
  1. 7-day refund eligibility
  2. Payment or service problems
  3. How to request a refund
  4. Processing and plan adjustments
  5. Payment terms and consumer rights

Request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have not used the paid benefits of that order. Duplicate charges, failure to activate a paid plan and service failures are reviewed separately and are not automatically excluded by this standard rule.

1. 7-day refund eligibility

This policy covers live purchases of Monthly Plus, Monthly Pro, Lifetime Plus and Lifetime Pro through Texta. Request a full refund from support within 7 × 24 hours of successful payment if you have not used the paid generation benefits associated with that order. Merely signing in, viewing plans or browsing existing materials is not use of paid generation benefits.

We review order timing, activation and relevant generation records. If you purchased in error, stop using paid benefits and contact us promptly. Ordinary change-of-mind requests after use or after 7 days are assessed individually without a promise of automatic refund. Applicable statutory return, withdrawal or other refund rights remain unaffected.

2. Payment or service problems

  • Duplicate charges or purchases: provide the affected orders so we can verify and coordinate return of the duplicate amount.
  • Paid but not activated: check the Texta account used to initiate the purchase and the payment-confirmation status. If paid benefits were not delivered, we will activate them or coordinate a refund.
  • Service failure preventing use of purchased benefits: provide the time and issue details. We will investigate and discuss repair, compensation or an appropriate refund based on the impact.
  • Suspected fraud or unauthorized transactions: promptly contact FastSpring Buyer Support and your payment provider, and also notify Texta.

3. How to request a refund

Email 1963372275@qq.com, preferably with the subject “Texta refund request”, and include:

  • Your Texta account email;
  • The FastSpring order ID or reference from your confirmation email;
  • The plan purchased, payment time and amount;
  • The reason and whether you have used the order’s paid benefits.

You may also submit payment or refund issues through FastSpring Buyer Support. Do not send full card numbers, security codes, Alipay passwords or identity-document photos. An order reference and account email are usually enough to begin reviewing the request.

4. Processing and plan adjustments

Texta reviews benefits and eligibility and coordinates the refund with FastSpring. As Merchant of Record, FastSpring processes the actual refund, normally through the original payment route. Timing depends on FastSpring, the payment method and the bank; immediate receipt is not guaranteed. Real-time methods such as Alipay require FastSpring Support to process the refund.

We communicate the review outcome or progress by email and explain any request for further order information. Once a refund is confirmed, the benefits granted by that purchase are revoked or adjusted; valid benefits from separate, unrefunded purchases remain unaffected. Where a partial refund applies, the amount and associated benefit adjustment are explained first.

5. Payment terms and consumer rights

FastSpring’s FastSpring Terms of Sale and applicable regional addenda govern the payment transaction. This policy explains Texta service remedies and voluntary refunds; it cannot limit mandatory consumer rights or prevent FastSpring from handling fraud, erroneous charges or disputed payments under its rules.

Monthly plans are one-time payments with no automatic renewal, so no automatic charge needs to be canceled. Lifetime plans are one-time payments with no preset expiry; this does not mean they are non-refundable in every circumstance. Purchases are currently unavailable; Test orders do not charge real funds and are not eligible for real-money refunds.

Operating brand: Texta, individually operated in China. Support: 1963372275@qq.com.
