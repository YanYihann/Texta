隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日

账户与学习数据用于提供 Texta 服务。生成时，所需的词汇和上下文会发送给 AI 服务商；付款信息由 FastSpring 处理。请勿在词汇、文章或导入文件中提交敏感个人信息。

1. 适用范围与联系方式

本政策适用于 Texta 网站和后端学习服务。Texta 是在中国由个人运营的品牌。与个人信息、访问、更正、删除或投诉有关的问题，请联系 1963372275@qq.com。FastSpring 付款页面及其他外部服务也适用其各自的隐私规则。

2. 我们处理的数据

  • 账户：邮箱、用户名、加盐密码哈希、账户标识、登录会话、角色、套餐及到期时间。我们不在数据库中保存明文密码。
  • 学习：提交的词汇、生成请求及上下文、文章、翻译、词汇解释、收藏夹、生词本、掌握状态和使用记录。收藏夹、生词本及相关偏好可同步至后端；浏览器也保存部分学习数据。
  • 支付：Texta 订单编号、所选套餐、金额、时间、支付状态及 FastSpring 订单或会话标识等用于开通和核对订单的数据。银行卡号、安全码及完整支付凭据在 FastSpring 页面输入，Texta 不直接收集这些卡片数据。历史人工充值申请若存在，可能包括当时提交的付款说明或凭证。
  • 运行与客服：网络请求、错误及必要的安全日志可能含 IP 地址、设备或浏览器信息；联系客服时，我们处理你提供的邮箱、问题描述及必要的订单信息。

3. 处理目的与必要性

我们使用这些数据完成注册登录、生成和展示学习内容、同步资料、计算每日积分、开通套餐、处理退款与客服请求、排查故障和防止滥用。生成需要提交相应词汇和上下文；不提供这些输入就无法完成该次生成。邮箱、密码和账户资料用于登录及保护账户。

处理依据包括履行你请求的服务、你对可选处理的同意以及适用法律要求。需要单独同意的处理会另行征求，而不是通过本政策一概视为授权。我们不将你的学习内容出售给广告商，当前网站没有接入广告追踪分析工具。

4. 服务商与境外处理

服务商 / 功能可能处理的数据与用途
GitHub Pages托管网站静态页面，处理访问该网站所需的网络请求。
Render / Neon托管 API 和数据库，处理账户、学习、用量及订单数据。当前数据库部署在美国地区。
302.AI 及其下游模型服务商生成请求中的词汇、指令、必要上下文或待翻译文本，以生成文章和解释。Texta 不向生成接口发送账户密码或银行卡信息。
Datamuse拼写候选查询时接收待查询的单词片段。

浏览器 / 系统语音服务点击发音时，由设备或浏览器所配置的语音服务处理朗读文本；某些语音可能使用网络服务。

这些服务可能在中国境外处理或存储数据，因此生成、云同步和付款可能涉及跨境传输。请仅提交完成学习所需的内容，不要输入身份证、健康信息、银行卡资料、私密通信或他人的个人信息。境外处理应符合适用的法定要求；需要单独同意或其他手续时，我们会另行办理。

Texta 没有自行建立使用用户学习内容训练模型的流程，但不能笼统保证所有下游模型服务商均不留存数据或不用于改进模型；其保存和使用方式受各自条款约束。如你需要严格保密，请不要提交该内容。

5. 浏览器存储、保存与安全

网站使用 localStorage 保存登录令牌、主题和语言、学习偏好、部分历史与收藏缓存及待付款订单标识。这些内容可能在关闭浏览器后继续保留。退出登录可结束本机登录状态，但不会自动清除所有学习缓存；共享设备使用后请退出并通过浏览器设置清除 Texta 的网站数据。清除本地数据不会自动删除云端数据。

账户及云端学习资料在提供服务所需期间保存。你可申请删除；交易、反欺诈或争议相关记录可能在法律或必要业务要求的期限内保留。部分同步删除采用删除标记，数据库备份和技术日志可能晚于当前可见数据清除；我们不会承诺点击删除后所有副本立即消失。

我们使用 HTTPS、密码加盐哈希及服务端访问控制等措施。服务商依其机制维护日志和备份。任何互联网服务都不能保证绝对安全；发现账号或数据安全问题请立即联系我们。

6. 你的选择与数据权利

你可通过 1963372275@qq.com 请求访问、更正、导出或删除个人信息、注销账户、撤回可选处理的同意，或就数据处理提出异议和投诉。我们会核实账户归属，以适用法律要求处理并告知结果；请不要在初次邮件中发送身份证或银行卡。

可在产品中删除学习条目，也可清除浏览器本地数据、停止生成或同步、停止使用服务。账户注销或删除不会自动触发退款，也不会删除 FastSpring 独立保存的合法交易记录；付款数据请求可同时向 FastSpring 提出。

7. 未成年人、更新与外部政策

未成年人应在监护人知情与同意下使用。不满 14 周岁儿童不应自行注册或提交个人信息；涉及儿童信息的处理需要监护人同意和适当保护。如果你认为我们收到未经授权的儿童信息，请联系删除。

重要隐私变化会在网站或账户邮箱通知，页面日期标识当前版本。外部付款政策：FastSpring 隐私政策；其他服务商的条款不由 Texta 控制，政策更新不会自动授权新的非必要用途。

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: October 5, 2026

What we collect, why we process it, and how you can manage your data.

Account and learning data support Texta’s features. Vocabulary and necessary context are sent to AI providers when you request generation. FastSpring handles payment information. Avoid sensitive personal information in words, articles or imported files.

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the Texta website and backend learning service. Texta is an individually operated brand in China. For personal-data questions, access, correction, deletion or complaints, contact 1963372275@qq.com. FastSpring checkout and other external services also have their own privacy rules.

2. Data we process

  • Account: email, username, salted password hash, account ID, login sessions, role, plan and expiry. We do not store plaintext passwords in the database.
  • Learning: submitted words, generation requests and context, articles, translations, explanations, favorites, notebook entries, mastery preferences and usage records. Favorites, notebooks and related preferences can sync to the backend; some learning data also remains in your browser.
  • Payment: Texta order ID, selected plan, amount, timestamps, payment status, and FastSpring order or session identifiers used for fulfillment and reconciliation. Card numbers, security codes and full payment credentials are entered on FastSpring’s page; Texta does not collect these card details directly. Historical manual-payment requests, where present, may include previously supplied payment notes or evidence.
  • Operations and support: network requests, errors and necessary security logs may include IP address and device or browser information. Support requests include the email, issue description and necessary order details you provide.

3. Purposes and necessity

We use this data for registration and login, content generation and display, syncing, daily credit accounting, plan activation, refunds and support, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Generation requires the relevant vocabulary and context; without these inputs that request cannot be completed. Email, password and account information support login and account protection.

The basis for processing includes providing the service you request, consent for optional processing and applicable legal requirements. Where separate consent is needed, we request it rather than treating this policy as blanket authorization. We do not sell learning content to advertisers; the current website does not use advertising tracking analytics.

4. Providers and international processing

Provider / featureData and purpose
GitHub PagesHosts static web pages and processes requests required to access the site.
Render / NeonHosts the API and database for account, learning, usage and order data. The current database is deployed in a US region.
302.AI and downstream model providersReceives words, instructions, necessary context or translation text to generate articles and explanations. Texta does not send account passwords or card details to the generation API.

These services may process or store data outside China, so generation, cloud syncing and payment can involve international transfers. Submit only what is needed for learning, without identity documents, health information, card data, private correspondence or other people’s personal information. International processing must meet applicable legal requirements; separate consent or other formalities will be addressed where required.

Texta does not itself run a model-training pipeline using your learning content. We cannot broadly guarantee that every downstream model provider neither retains data nor uses it to improve models; their processing follows their respective terms. Do not submit content that requires strict confidentiality.

5. Browser storage, retention and security

We use localStorage for login tokens, theme and language, learning preferences, some history and favorites caches, and pending-payment identifiers. These can persist after you close the browser. Signing out ends the local signed-in state but does not automatically clear every learning cache. On shared devices, sign out and clear Texta site data in browser settings. Clearing local data does not automatically delete cloud data.

Account and cloud learning data are retained for as long as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion. Transaction, anti-fraud or dispute records may be retained for legally required or necessary business periods. Some sync deletions use deletion markers; backup and technical-log removal may occur after data disappears from the current interface. We do not promise that every copy vanishes immediately upon a delete action.

We use HTTPS, salted password hashing and server-side access controls. Providers maintain logs and backups under their own systems. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; contact us promptly about account or data-security issues.

6. Your choices and rights

Email 1963372275@qq.com to request access, correction, export or deletion of personal information, account closure, withdrawal of optional consent, or to raise objections or complaints. We verify account ownership, handle requests as required by applicable law and communicate the outcome. Do not send identity documents or card details in your initial email.

You can delete learning entries in the product, clear browser data, stop generation or syncing, or stop using the service. Account deletion does not automatically trigger a refund or delete transaction records independently retained by FastSpring. Payment-data requests may also be directed to FastSpring.

7. Minors, updates and external policies

Minors should use Texta with a guardian’s knowledge and consent. Children under 14 should not independently register or submit personal information; processing children’s data requires guardian consent and appropriate safeguards. Contact us to remove children’s data you believe was received without authorization.

Material privacy changes will be communicated on the website or by account email; the page date identifies the current version. External payment policy: FastSpring Privacy Policy. Texta does not control other providers’ terms, and policy updates do not automatically authorize new non-essential uses.
